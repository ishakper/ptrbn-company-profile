# Page Objectives
## PT. Pelayaran Nasional Radhika Bahari Nusantara — Web Platform

> Date: 2026-08-02 | Version: 1.0.0 | Status: APPROVED

---

## 1. Services Page Objective (`/services`)

The core objective of the Services page is to act as a **high-conversion B2B selling tool** rather than a passive list.

### A. Strategic Goals
1. **Lead Generation (Primary):** Convert logistics managers, manufacturers, and bulk commodity shippers into qualified inbound inquiry leads via direct contact buttons.
2. **Value Differentiation:** Communicate PT. RBN's unique capabilities (punctual shipping schedules, real-time cargo updates, specialized bulk cargo handling).
3. **Operational Clarity:** Detail specialized logistics categories (Containerized Cargo, Bulk Cargo, Project heavy-lifts, Port Agency) so shippers quickly identify RBN's compatibility with their cargo profile.

### B. Page Success Metrics
* Click-through rate (CTR) to the Contact Inquiry form (`/contact`).
* Duration of user engagement on individual service category details.
* B2B inquiry form completion rates.

---

## 2. Fleet Page Objective (`/fleet`)

The core objective of the Fleet page is to establish **operational credibility, scale, and compliance** rather than being just an inventory of ships.

### A. Strategic Goals
1. **Credibility & Trust (Primary):** Prove that PT. RBN operates a legitimate, modern, and highly compliant shipping fleet inspected by BKI (Biro Klasifikasi Indonesia).
2. **Capacity Validation:** Demonstrate heavy-cargo capability by displaying Gross Tonnage (GRT) metrics, Deadweight Tonnage (DWT), container TEU layout configurations, and specialized vessel types.
3. **Safety Assurance:** Emphasize crew certifications, safety inspection schedules, and real-time tracking metrics (GPS/AIS) to build shipper confidence.

### B. Page Success Metrics
* Download rates of vessel technical specification datasheets.
* Interaction rates with vessel inventory filtering mechanisms.

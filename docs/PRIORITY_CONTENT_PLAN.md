## 3. Detailed Action Items

### Category 1: Critical (Must be implemented before staging release)
- **Legal Compliance Pages:** Draft and publish *Privacy Policy* (complying with Indonesian PDP law) and *Terms of Service* for maritime transport.
- **Vessel Specs & Registration:** Build the SQL database schema and Filament resources to register the 48 vessels with their exact Gross Tonnage, Deadweight, and BKI (Biro Klasifikasi Indonesia) certification.
- **WhatsApp Support Link:** Integrate a floating WhatsApp button with pre-filled inquiry text pointing to the customer service hotline.

### Category 2: High (Required for launch)
- **Core Services Detail Content:** Write 300-word detailed descriptions explaining cargo tracking capabilities, route schedules, and port agent duties.
- **Active Vacancies List:** Add vacancy resource panels to Filament to display ship crew and corporate office positions.
- **Port Listing & Transit Times:** Define route transit times (e.g. Surabaya to Banjarmasin transit duration) on the shipping routes page.

### Category 3: Medium (Post-launch updates)
- **Milestone Timeline narrative:** Write an in-depth company history showing milestones from 1999 to 2026.
- **Client Project Case Studies:** Write 3 case studies highlighting difficult out-of-gauge shipping tasks or heavy-lift bulk cargo transports.
- **News articles:** Publish 3 official press releases detailing route network updates.

### Category 4: Low (Long-term roadmap)
- **Operational Video:** Film and host a 2-minute corporate video profile on YouTube/Vimeo to embed on the homepage.
- **Onboard and Office Photography:** Coordinate photos of vessel operations and Surabaya headquarters.

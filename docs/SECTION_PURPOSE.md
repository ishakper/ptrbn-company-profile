## 1. Services Page Sections (`/services`)

Every section must drive user comprehension and B2B engagement.

### Section 1: Hero Banner
* **Visual Layout:** Subtle light gray grid, badge.
* **Content:** Display title: "B2B Maritime Cargo Logistics & Shipping Solutions".
* **Strategic Purpose:** Confirms immediate relevance to corporate cargo shippers.

### Section 2: Core Shipping Categories Grid
* **Visual Layout:** 3-column white shadow cards.
* **Content:** Detailed cards for *Container Shipping*, *Bulk Cargo Logistics*, and *Project/Heavy-Lift Shipping*.
* **Strategic Purpose:** Enables quick selection of service classes, pointing directly to technical specifications.

### Section 3: Operational Safety & Quality Advantages
* **Visual Layout:** 2-column split with text list on the left and a mock certification list on the right.
* **Content:** Detail RBN's ISO standards, cargo care protocols, and zero-accident target policies.
* **Strategic Purpose:** Overcomes trust hurdles regarding cargo damage risks.

### Section 4: Booking & Delivery Workflow
* **Visual Layout:** 4-step horizontal process timeline.
* **Content:** Step 1: Inquiry & Specs -> Step 2: Quotation Approval -> Step 3: Vessel Loading -> Step 4: Cargo Transit.
* **Strategic Purpose:** Educates users on the simplicity and transparency of working with RBN Group.

## 2. Fleet Page Sections (`/fleet`)

Every section must establish heavy-lift capacity and regulatory compliance.

### Section 1: Hero Banner
* **Visual Layout:** Branded clean header banner.
* **Content:** Display title: "Our Fleet — Maritime Power & Archipelago Connectivity".
* **Strategic Purpose:** Sets the tone for corporate capabilities.

### Section 2: Fleet Composition Overview
* **Visual Layout:** 4-card statistics row.
* **Content:** Cargo Vessels, Container Ships, Bulk Carriers, and specialized Tankers.
* **Strategic Purpose:** Establishes scale (48 active vessels).

### Section 3: Vessel Spec Inventory Grid
* **Visual Layout:** Responsive table containing searchable columns.
* **Content:** Vessel Name, Gross Tonnage, Deadweight, BKI Classification, and Status.
* **Strategic Purpose:** Empowers logistics managers to search and verify technical cargo specs against their requirements.

### Section 4: Classification & Quality Standards
* **Visual Layout:** 2-column details.
* **Content:** Biro Klasifikasi Indonesia (BKI) audit certificates and port safety clearances.
* **Strategic Purpose:** Overcomes regulatory and safety trust hurdles.

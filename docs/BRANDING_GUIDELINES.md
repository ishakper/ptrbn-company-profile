## 2. Logo Integration Standard (No-Wrapper Rule)

To protect the elegance of the brand, do not apply containment styling:
- **No Card Fills:** Do not frame the logo inside a white pill, background box, or rounded container.
- **No Borders or Separators:** The logo links directly to the root path without decorative borders.
- **Original Colors:** Do not apply `dark:brightness-0 dark:invert` or any color changes. The official navy blue, metallic gradient, and chrome borders must be displayed intact.
- **Background Adaptability:** For low contrast transparent overlays, use clean gray drop-shadow styling inside the img element. Scrolled states use solid background canvas for high readability.

## 3. Typography System

- **Primary Typeface:** `Inter` (Standard body copy, lists, controls).
- **Secondary Typeface:** `Plus Jakarta Sans` (Display headings, titles, active numbers).
- **Monospace Typeface:** `JetBrains Mono` (Dynamic logs, coordinates, ship parameters).

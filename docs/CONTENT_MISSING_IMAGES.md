# RBN Content Gap & Missing Media Report
**PT. Pelayaran Nasional Radhika Bahari Nusantara**

This audit reviews current photographic coverage of RBN operations and identifies critical gaps that should be addressed in future professional photoshoots.

---

## 1. Current Asset Coverage (High Quality)

We have high-quality authentic coverage for:
- Landing Craft Utility (LCU) vessel sailing and port docking.
- Drydock propeller/rudder engineering inspection.
- Vessel passenger cabin interior with CSR/education visits.
- Onboard crew/management group photo on the deck.

---

## 2. Identified Media Gaps & Target List

To elevate the corporate B2B identity to match global brands like Maersk or NYK Line, we recommend commissioning professional shoots for the following areas:

### A. Operational Bridgeroom & Navigation
- **Focus:** Officers at the control console, radar screen overlays, steering columns, and navigational charts.
- **Purpose:** Illustrates professional seamanship and advanced technological instrumentation.

### B. Vessel Engine Room & Power Systems
- **Focus:** Power generators, main engine valves, fuel filters, and engineers holding gauges.
- **Purpose:** Highlights mechanical reliability, safety standards, and robust maintenance protocols.

### C. Commercial Office & Client Relations
- **Focus:** Customer support agents answering calls, logistics planners reviewing routes on dual screens, and team discussions in the boardroom.
- **Purpose:** Illustrates customer-centric service and smooth office operations.

### D. Drone & Night Operations
- **Focus:** Aerial drone shots of vessel fleets sailing in formations, and night loading operations under port spotlight beams.
- **Purpose:** Generates premium marketing visual hooks for the landing page hero section.
